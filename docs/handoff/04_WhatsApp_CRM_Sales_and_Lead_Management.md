## Purpose
WhatsApp should function as a guided concierge and conversion channel, not as an uncontrolled inbox or spam bot. It should help the team respond faster, capture context once, route each lead to an owner and preserve the relationship as the traveller moves from curiosity to preparation.
The website remains the structured source of truth. WhatsApp brings that information into a conversational context and introduces a responsible human at the right time.
| Operating principle: Automation should remove repetition and delay. It should not imitate authority, pressure devotion, conceal that it is automated or make high-stakes commitments. |

## Channel model

### Supported entry points
- Website hero and journey-page WhatsApp CTAs
- Live-status notification opt-in
- Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns
- Instagram and Facebook profile links
- QR codes on printed material, events or partner locations
- Referral links from completed travellers
- Manual lead creation from calls, email and walk-ins
- International enquiries from email or web form that continue on WhatsApp when appropriate
Every digital entry point should carry available context such as journey, departure, campaign, page, language and intent. The customer should not have to repeat which package they were viewing.

### Channel separation
Use distinct purposes even if one team handles them:
| Channel purpose | Example | Required behaviour |
| New sales | Public WhatsApp CTA | Qualification and sales-owner assignment |
| Active booking support | Booking-aware contact | Recognise booking and route to the trip team |
| During-trip urgent support | Dedicated coordinator or emergency path | High-priority human response, not a marketing flow |
| Vendor operations | Separate operational channel | Do not mix traveller sales with supplier coordination |
| Broadcast updates | Opted-in templates | Only relevant, consented and policy-compliant messages |

## Consent and trust requirements
- Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform or an authorised provider.
- Do not scrape Instagram users or send unsolicited bulk messages.
- Record the source and timestamp of communication consent.
- State why the user is being messaged and how to stop nonessential updates.
- Separate essential booking or trip communications from promotional consent.
- Do not add a person to a broadcast list merely because they asked one question.
- Never request passport, medical or identity documents through an insecure public link.
- Make human escalation visible.

## Conversation design principles
1. Open warmly and identify the service. Do not pretend a bot is a person.
2. Ask a small number of high-value questions. Avoid a twenty-question interrogation.
3. Reflect understanding. Summarise the group and intent before recommending a next step.
4. Use approved, current content. Pull itinerary, status, price and policy from the system of record.
5. Offer a human at moments of uncertainty. Health concerns, private groups, payment, complaints and route disruption should not remain fully automated.
6. Keep the devotee's language dignified. A greeting such as "Har Har Mahadev" may be offered in the Indian devotional flow, but international or non-devotional visitors should receive a welcoming neutral alternative.
7. Do not pressure through religious belief. Avoid countdowns, guilt or divine claims.
8. Preserve context across channels. Sales should see the website page, questions asked and summary before calling.

## Initial greeting and language flow
A suggested structure:
1. Welcome and brand identification
2. Disclosure that the first questions are automated, where required
3. Language preference: English, Hindi or request for another language
4. Primary intent:
- Adi Kailash and Om Parvat
- Kumaon spiritual circuit
- Private or custom journey
- Current route or permit status
- Existing booking support
5. Option to speak to a person
The flow should adapt. A user asking only whether permits are open should receive the current verified answer and subscription option before being pushed into a full sales questionnaire.

## Lead qualification flow

### Essential qualification fields
Collect progressively rather than all at once.
| Field | Why it matters | Notes |
| Journey interest | Routes the lead and content | May be unknown at first |
| Preferred date or date window | Matches departure and urgency | Capture flexibility |
| Group size | Determines tier, availability and economics | Allow estimated group size |
| Starting city or gateway | Determines transfer and package scope | Do not assume Delhi |
| Age range or senior-inclusive group | Helps plan pace and human consultation | Do not make medical decisions |
| Shared, comfort or private preference | Reveals service expectations | Explain concrete differences |
| Language | Improves communication | Store preferred language |
| Budget comfort, when appropriate | Avoids unsuitable recommendations | Ask carefully after value context |
| International or domestic | Changes documents, payments and gateway support | Ask country/time zone if international |
| Primary concern | Enables a useful human response | Examples: altitude, parents, dates, rooms, permits |
| Call preference | Creates a clear next action | Time slot and time zone |

### Optional later fields
- Number of rooms and occupancy preference
- Food or accessibility needs voluntarily disclosed
- Previous high-altitude experience
- Interest in extensions
- Referral source or referring traveller
- Preferred communication channel beyond WhatsApp
Do not collect detailed medical history during lead qualification. A high-level concern can trigger a human planning call and reviewed medical-readiness guidance.

## Lead summary
At the end of qualification, create a concise structured summary visible to the customer and sales owner.
Example:
| You are planning an Adi Kailash and Om Parvat journey for four family members from Delhi, preferably in the second half of October. One traveller is a senior, and your main concerns are pace, room quality and route certainty. You prefer a comfort or private option. A journey guide will call you tomorrow between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM. |
The customer should be able to correct the summary.

## Lead lifecycle
Use explicit stages with clear entry and exit criteria.
| Stage | Meaning | Required next action |
| New | Lead created but not reviewed | Assign owner and initial response |
| Contacted | Team has made a meaningful first contact | Qualify or schedule follow-up |
| Qualified | Core fit and intent are understood | Recommend journey, date or consultation |
| Consultation scheduled | Call or meeting booked | Prepare context and attend |
| Proposal shared | Customer has a defined offer and conditions | Follow up by agreed date |
| Reservation invited | Customer may make the approved conditional or confirmed payment | Track payment window and questions |
| Reserved | Reservation amount received and acknowledged | Start onboarding and condition tracking |
| Confirmed | Booking meets approved payment and departure conditions | Handoff to traveller preparation and operations |
| Nurture | Interested but not ready, date unavailable or route closed | Send consented, relevant updates |
| Lost | Customer chose not to proceed or is unsuitable | Record reason and stop pressure |
| Cancelled or refunded | A prior reservation ended | Complete finance and service recovery |
| Completed traveller | Journey finished | Feedback, review, referral and future relationship |
Avoid a stage called simply "Hot" without a defined customer state. Sales priority can be a separate score or flag.

## Lead ownership and service levels
Every active lead should have one accountable owner. Team members may collaborate, but ownership should not disappear between sales and operations.

### Suggested response expectations
These are management targets to refine, not automated promises unless the team can meet them.
| Situation | Target behaviour |
| New WhatsApp enquiry during support hours | Automated acknowledgement immediately; human review quickly |
| High-intent departure or payment question | Priority human response |
| Callback request | Confirmed time window and owner |
| International enquiry | Time-zone-aware acknowledgement and consultation options |
| Existing booking issue | Route directly to booking or trip owner |
| During-trip urgent message | Immediate escalation path and human acknowledgement |
| Complaint | Named owner, acknowledgement and resolution timeline |
The product should measure actual response and resolution time. It should not display a response promise that the team routinely misses.

## Human takeover rules
Automation should transfer to a human when any of the following occurs:
- Customer asks to speak to a person
- Payment, refund, cancellation or price exception is discussed
- Traveller raises health, altitude or fitness concerns
- Group includes seniors, young children or accessibility needs that require planning
- Private, international or large-group itinerary is requested
- Route status is disputed, unclear or stale
- Customer expresses distress, anger or a complaint
- Customer asks for a guarantee about permits, weather, darshan, rooms or safety
- Automation fails to understand two consecutive messages
- Existing booking or during-trip support is detected
- A legal, insurance or regulatory question is raised
The human should receive the lead summary, conversation history, relevant journey, current status and open question.

## Sales workspace
The sales team needs a practical workspace rather than a raw contact list.

### Primary views
- New leads requiring response
- Calls due today
- Qualified leads without next action
- Proposals awaiting follow-up
- Reservation invitations expiring soon
- Route-closed or date-unavailable nurture leads
- International and private enquiries
- Existing booking messages accidentally entering sales
- Lost reasons and campaign quality

### Lead detail view
Show at a glance:
- Customer summary
- Journey and date context
- Group composition
- Source and campaign
- Stage, owner and next action
- Conversation history
- Calls and notes
- Proposal and policy version
- Current route/departure status
- Reservation or booking link
- Sensitive-data warnings and permissions

### Manager view
- Unassigned leads
- Leads breaching response target
- Stage ageing
- Follow-up completion
- Conversion by owner, source and journey
- Loss reasons
- Duplicate leads
- Customer complaints or escalations

## Approved message categories

### Informational
- Journey overview
- Current route status
- Service-tier explanation
- Documents and preparation guide
- Accommodation reality
- Gateway and travel guidance

### Transactional
- Callback confirmation
- Proposal sent
- Reservation acknowledgement
- Payment receipt or failure
- Document task reminder
- Departure update
- Driver, stay or pickup information
- Reschedule, refund or cancellation update

### Nurture
- Permit or season opening notification
- New departure announcement
- Relevant readiness content
- Founder or field update
- Extension opportunity

### Post-trip
- Welcome back
- Feedback request
- Complaint-resolution follow-up
- Review request after service issues are addressed
- Referral invitation
- Future journey suggestion
Promotional broadcasts require valid consent and frequency discipline. Transactional messages should not hide unrelated promotions.

## Proposal workflow
A proposal should be a versioned commercial and service summary, not a casual price sent in chat.

### Required proposal content
- Customer or group context
- Journey, tier and date or date window
- Gateway and pickup scope
- Itinerary summary
- Concrete service differences
- Accommodation and room assumptions
- Inclusions and exclusions
- Price, taxes and payment schedule as approved
- Conditional elements such as permit or minimum group
- Cancellation, rescheduling and disruption reference
- Proposal validity
- Operating entity or partner
- Named contact
The system should preserve which proposal the customer accepted. A later price or itinerary edit should not silently rewrite the historical record.

## Reservation and booking transition
Before inviting payment, the system must know:
- Whether the action is a protected reservation, waitlist payment, deposit or confirmed booking
- What conditions apply
- What capacity is being held and for how long
- Which policy version applies
- Who receives and invoices the payment
- What happens if the departure is not confirmed
After payment:
- Acknowledge the exact state in writing
- Link the lead to the reservation or booking
- Assign the preparation owner
- Display the next required action
- Stop conflicting sales nurture
- Preserve finance and message records

## Nurture and lost-lead management
A lead may be valuable even when the immediate departure is unavailable.

### Nurture reasons
- Route or permits not open
- Preferred date not available
- Customer waiting for family confirmation
- Price timing or budget
- Needs a slower or private trip
- International planning window
- Interested in future season
- Not yet medically or logistically ready
Nurture should be topic-specific. A route-status subscriber should not receive generic daily promotions. A customer who declined because of parents' pace may receive a slower-itinerary update, not a discount blast.

### Loss reasons
- Chose competitor
- Price mismatch
- Date mismatch
- Trust concern
- Route uncertainty
- Comfort mismatch
- No response
- Group cancelled
- Not suitable after consultation
- Duplicate or invalid lead
Loss reasons should inform product and campaign decisions, not punish salespeople for honest qualification.

## International lead handling
International enquiries may require:
- Country and time-zone-aware scheduling
- Email summary in addition to WhatsApp
- Clear English explanations of local terms
- Airport, railway and gateway planning
- Passport, visa and permit guidance reviewed by operations
- Travel-insurance expectations
- International payment and currency context
- Private guide and cultural orientation options
- Longer planning horizon
- Emergency and family-contact clarity
Do not automatically show domestic package assumptions to an international customer without context.

## AI assistance boundaries
AI may:
- Classify intent and language
- Summarise the conversation for a human
- Suggest an approved FAQ answer
- Recommend the most relevant journey or guide based on structured criteria
- Draft follow-up messages for review
- Detect missing next actions or overdue leads
- Translate routine content when reviewed
AI may not independently:
- Confirm route, permit or departure status without an approved current source
- Provide medical clearance or diagnosis
- Change price, policy, refund or itinerary commitments
- Promise a specific hotel, vehicle or view
- Resolve a complaint without human ownership
- Infer sensitive health or religious information beyond what the customer voluntarily states
- Fabricate urgency or social proof

## Acceptance scenarios
The sales and WhatsApp experience is ready when:
1. A website visitor starts WhatsApp from an Adi Kailash page and the sales team can see the journey and source.
2. A status-only question receives the current verified status without forcing full qualification.
3. A family lead can provide group size, dates, origin and concerns in a short flow and request a call.
4. A high-altitude health concern triggers a human handoff without medical advice from the bot.
5. Every active lead has an owner and a next action.
6. A proposal can be tied to a lead and later to a reservation without losing the accepted version.
7. A route-closed lead can opt into relevant updates without being placed in an uncontrolled promotional list.
8. An international enquiry is routed with country, time zone and private-service context.
9. A confirmed booking stops inappropriate sales follow-ups and enters traveller preparation.
10. Managers can identify response delays, stage ageing, loss reasons and conversion by source.
