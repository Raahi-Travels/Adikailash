# Sacred North — Working-Brand Product Handoff Pack

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 8 August 2026  
**Status:** Internal product and delivery handoff  
**Brand status:** “The Sacred North” is a provisional working name and must remain replaceable through configuration until legal, domain and trademark clearance is complete.

## Purpose

This pack translates the current vision for a technology-enabled Himalayan spiritual-travel company into an implementation-ready managerial brief. It is intended for founders, product managers, designers, coding agents, content teams, growth teams and operations leaders.

The documents define the product outcomes, customer experience, core workflows, information architecture, operational truth, trust rules and delivery priorities. They intentionally avoid prescribing a final framework, database, hosting provider, messaging vendor or detailed system design.

## Recommended reading order

1. **00 — Master Handoff Index**: scope, assumptions, priorities and how to use the pack.
2. **01 — Product Vision, Customer and Service Blueprint**: customer psychology, proposition, service model and success outcomes.
3. **02 — Brand, UI/UX and Content Language**: provisional identity, visual direction, voice and religious-sensitivity rules.
4. **03 — Public Website**: information architecture, conversion paths, live status and content requirements.
5. **04 — WhatsApp, CRM, Sales and Lead Management**: enquiry qualification, consent, sales ownership and human handoff.
6. **05 — Traveller Portal and Trip Companion**: reservation, documents, payments, preparation and during-trip experience.
7. **06 — Admin, Operations, Vendor and Departure Management**: operational control centre, readiness, suppliers, incidents and reconciliation.
8. **07 — Growth, SEO, AEO, Content and Analytics**: demand generation, attribution and local-authority strategy.
9. **08 — Technical Architecture and Data Overview**: implementation-agnostic capability, domain, integration, AI, security and resilience map.
10. **09 — Delivery Roadmap, Governance, Trust and Compliance**: phased execution, founder roles, quality gates, risk and launch readiness.

## Suggested coding-agent assignment

Each coding-agent brief should reference:

- the relevant feature document;
- the master assumptions and non-negotiables in document 00;
- the brand and content rules in document 02;
- shared state, data and integration direction in document 08; and
- the Definition of Ready, Definition of Done and trust rules in document 09.

Agents should propose implementation choices and trade-offs rather than silently inventing unresolved business policy. Brand strings, legal-entity identity, operator disclosure, policies, contact details, destination data and status labels should remain configurable.

## Product north star

The platform should help a traveller move from spiritual intent to an honestly represented, safely coordinated and locally grounded journey. Emotional storytelling may create resonance, but operational truth must control dates, payments, route status, accommodation promises, documents and customer communications.

## Important boundaries

- The product must never imply that the company controls divine access, guarantees darshan, guarantees weather or certifies medical fitness.
- WhatsApp and other messaging must be consent-based, contextual and capable of human takeover.
- A public departure should never appear confirmed solely because a page, advertisement or payment link exists.
- Customer-facing information must distinguish verified, unverified, stale and booking-specific status.
- The product should support an operating-partner model until the company holds every registration required for the service being sold.
- High-altitude, transport, permit, refund, privacy and tourism obligations require professional review before production launch.
